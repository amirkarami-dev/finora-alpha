2 · First-time setup

do this once, in this order
Goal: fill the lists that every other page picks from. A new company starts completely empty.
Base InfoGoodsBanksCash safesExpense / Revenue categoriesCost centresWarehousePersonsReady
  1. Base Info › Goods → New. Add each product you trade: name, code, metal type (copper, aluminium …), form (cathode, ingot, scrap …), HS code if you know it.the goods list. These names appear when you add goods to a contract.
  2. Base Info › Banks → New. Add each company bank account: name, currency, account number, IBAN, SWIFT.
    Base Info › Cash safes → New. Add each cash box: name and currency.you will pick these when money comes in by bank transfer (TT) or when a cheque is paid.
  3. Base Info › Expense categories and Revenue categories → New. Example expenses: Freight, Customs, Rent, Salaries. Example revenues: Commission, Interest. For each one choose the scope: Invoice (belongs to a shipment) or General (office cost).
  4. Base Info › Cost centres → New. Optional. Example: Dubai office, Iraq branch.
  5. Warehouse › Warehouses → New warehouse. Name, code, location. You need at least one active warehouse before you can confirm an invoice.
  6. Persons → add your buyers and suppliers (section 3).
  7. Settings → check the FX rate (AED per 1 USD) and IQD exchange rate. These are only the default values shown in new forms.

Rules for codes

  • Codes use letters, numbers and hyphens only. They become UPPERCASE.
  • A code cannot be changed after saving. Choose short and clear: CU-CATH, MW, AM.
  • A code cannot be used twice in the same list.

Nothing is deleted

  • Records with history cannot be deleted. Use Deactivate instead. An inactive record is hidden from the pick lists but stays in old documents.
  • Currency of a bank or cash safe cannot change after saving.

3 · Add a person

buyers, suppliers, employees
Goal: create the party you trade with, so you can make contracts and record money with them.
PersonsNew personFill the formSavePerson created
  1. Open Persons and click New person.
  2. Name — the company or person name, as it must appear on documents. Example: Alco Metal Trading.
  3. Code — short, example AM. It becomes part of every contract number of this person, so choose it carefully. It cannot change later.
  4. Type — choose one:
    TypeUse it forCan have contracts?
    Buyera customer who buys from youYes — sell contracts
    Suppliera company you buy fromYes — purchase contracts
    Bothbuys and sellsYes — both
    Employee / Otherstaff, landlord, bank … (for expenses and general payments)No
  5. Currency — USD, AED or IQD. This is the default for new forms with this person.
  6. For buyers and suppliers: Payment terms (days) — how many days they have to pay. After this, an invoice counts as overdue. Credit limit in USD — the app warns when a buyer goes above it.
  7. Contact, email, phone, country — optional.
  8. Customer portal access — tick this only for the ONE customer that will sign in to My Account (section 14).
  9. Click Save."Person created". The person is in the list with 0 balance.

Partners (only for purchase deals with profit sharing)

  1. Partners → New: name and code. Later, on a purchase contract, you give each partner a % of a goods line. The total must be 100 or less; your company keeps the rest.

Good to know

  • Click a person's row to see their balance, contracts, invoices and payments (section 12).
  • To edit, open the person and click Edit person.

Cannot save?

  • "This code is already in use" — pick another code.
  • "Letters, numbers and hyphens only" — remove spaces and symbols from the code.

4 · Make a contract and add goods

the deal
Goal: write down the deal: who, when, where to, and what goods at what price.
ContractsNew sell / purchase contractHeaderAdd goodsContract ACTIVE
  1. Open Contracts. Choose the tab Sell contracts or Purchase contracts, then click New sell contract or New purchase contract.
  2. Customer — pick the person. A sell contract shows only buyers; a purchase contract shows only suppliers.
  3. Date, Destination (example: NINGBO), notes. Click Save."Contract created". The contract number is made from the person code and the date, example AM-P-251101156. Status is ACTIVE.
  4. Open the contract and click Add goods. For each goods line:
    FieldWhat to enter
    Productstart typing; pick from your Goods list so the spelling stays the same
    Quantity (MT)total metric tons in the deal
    LME fixed?Yes = the LME price is agreed now → enter Fixed LME price (USD/MT).
    No = floating; you enter the LME price later, on the invoice
    LME %the agreed percentage of LME, example 94.76
    PremiumUSD per MT added on top (can be 0 or negative)
    IncotermFOB, CIF, CFR, CNF, EXW or DAP
    Partnerspurchase only: partner + % share, optional
  5. Click Save. Repeat for every product in the deal.the goods table with the price per MT and the line value, both computed for you.

How the price is computed

  • Price per MT = fixed LME price × LME % ÷ 100 + premium
  • Line value = price per MT × quantity
  • Example: 11,685 × 94.76 % + 0 = 11,072 USD/MT

Two "remaining" numbers

  • Shipped / Remaining on the contract = goods that left in containers.
  • Uninvoiced on the Purchase / Sale pages = goods not yet on a confirmed document.
  • They are different things. Both start at the full quantity.

5 · Buying: order → provisional → invoice

Purchase page
Goal: record what you buy from a supplier, step by step, and put the goods into your warehouse.
PurchaseOrder (PO)Provisional (PP)Invoice (PI)Goods in warehouse

You can skip a step: an order can go straight to the invoice. You can also start directly with a provisional or an invoice.

A. Make the order

  1. Purchase › Orders → New order. Pick the contract; the supplier fills in by itself. Choose currency and rate. Save.a DRAFT document with a number like PO-2026-0001.
  2. Click Add items → Insert all from contract (or tick lines and Insert selected). Change quantities if this order is only part of the contract.
  3. Click Confirm.status CONFIRMED. The document is now locked. The page shows how many MT of the contract are still uninvoiced.

B. Convert to a provisional, then price it

  1. On the confirmed order click Convert → choose Provisional.a new DRAFT PP-2026-0001 with the same items. It shows "Reference: PO-2026-0001".
  2. In the Pricing panel enter the LME price and LME date (needed for floating lines). Click Apply to all, or set each line. Add a Discount % if agreed.every line now has an amount, and the total at the bottom.
  3. Optional: Send marks it as sent to the other party. Print gives a printable copy.
  4. Click Confirm.

C. Convert to the final invoice and receive the goods

  1. On the confirmed provisional click Convert → Invoice. Check prices (they are copied). Click Confirm.
  2. A window asks: which warehouse receives the goods? Choose it and click OK."Document confirmed", and in Warehouse › Documents a new receipt GRN-2026-0001. Stock goes up.

Good to know

  • A DRAFT can be edited freely. CONFIRMED is locked — to change it, cancel it and make a new one.
  • Each document can be converted only once. After that the Convert button disappears.

If the app refuses

  • "Quantity exceeds the contract's remaining uninvoiced amount" — another document already used that quantity. Lower the quantity.
  • "Cannot cancel — a converted document already exists" — cancel the newer document first, then this one.

6 · Selling: order → provisional → invoice

Sale page
Goal: record what you sell to a buyer and take the goods out of the warehouse. Same steps as buying, with one extra check: stock.
SaleOrder (SO)Provisional (SP)Invoice (SI)Stock checkGoods out · buyer owes you
  1. Sale › Orders → New order. Pick the sell contract. Save, Add items, Confirm.
  2. Convert → Provisional. Enter LME price, LME date, discount. Confirm.
  3. Optional but useful: on each line click Assign container and pick the container that carries these goods (section 7). The container number and BL number then print on the invoice.
  4. Convert → Invoice. While it is a DRAFT, the items table shows an "Available" column: how many MT of that product are in stock. Red means not enough.
  5. Click Confirm. A window asks: which warehouse do the goods leave from? Choose and click OK."Document confirmed", a new issue GDN-2026-0001 in Warehouse › Documents, stock goes down, and the buyer's balance goes up by the invoice total.
  6. Click Send to mark it sent, Print to print or save as PDF.

After the sale — what happens by itself

WhereWhat you will see
Persons › the buyerOutstanding balance goes up. It becomes Overdue after their payment-terms days.
DashboardTotal invoiced, outstanding and the aging chart update.
ContainersThe container linked to the invoice shows OPEN, then PAID when the invoice is fully paid, or OVERDUE.
My Account (portal)The customer sees the new open invoice.

Good to know

  • Stock is counted per warehouse and per product from confirmed receipts and issues only.
  • You can record a payment straight from the invoice page — the Payments card at the bottom.

If the app refuses

  • "Insufficient stock" with a product name and the available MT — receive the goods first (a purchase invoice, or Warehouse › New receipt), or lower the quantity. Nothing was changed.
  • "No active warehouse" — create one in Warehouse › Warehouses.

7 · Record a container

shipments
Goal: record each shipment with its container number, dates and papers, and link it to the goods it carries.
ContainersNew containerReference + datesAdd goods lineSaveShipped MT updates on the contract
  1. Open Containers → New container.
  2. Container — the container number, example MSNU8018095.
  3. Load date — when it was loaded. Arrival — expected or real arrival date. The list shows "Due in N days" or "Overdue by N days" from this.
  4. Papers: Bill of Lading no., Booking no., Seal no., Gross / Net weight (kg). All optional, but they print on documents.
  5. Click Add goods line → Choose goods from a contract → enter the MT in this container. The form shows "N MT left" on that contract line. One container can carry goods from several lines.
  6. Click Save."Container added". On the contract, the Shipped column goes up and Remaining goes down.

Link the container to an invoice

  1. Open the sale or purchase document, find the line, click Assign container. Only containers carrying that product are listed (tick "Show all containers" to see the rest).Container no. and BL no. on the line, and on the printed invoice.

Warehouse pages

TabWhat it is for
Warehousesthe list of your warehouses — add or edit them here
Inventorystock levels: MT of each product in each warehouse right now
Documentsevery receipt (IN, GRN-…) and issue (OUT, GDN-…), with the invoice that made it

Good to know

  • Receipts and issues are normally created by confirming an invoice (sections 5 and 6). You can also make one by hand: New receipt / New issue, choose the confirmed invoice and the lines.

Take care

  • A container has no money on it. Paid / overdue on a container comes from the invoice it is linked to.
  • A goods line that is already on a document cannot be removed from the container.

8 · Record a payment

money in, money out
Goal: record money you received from a buyer or paid to a supplier, and say which invoices it settles.
PaymentsNew invoice paymentHeaderAdd lineConfirmBalance updated

Which kind?

TabUse it whenResult
Invoice paymentsthe money is for one or more specific invoicesthose invoices show as paid (fully or partly)
General paymentsmoney with a person but no invoice — advance, deposit, loan, salarysits on the person's account as a balance
Chequessee section 9

Steps — invoice payment

  1. Payments › Invoice payments → New invoice payment.
  2. Person, Date, Money direction: Money in (received from a buyer) or Money out (paid to a supplier). Currency and Declared total — the amount on the bank slip. Save.a DRAFT payment. "Not yet allocated" shows the full amount.
  3. Click Add line. Pick the invoice, enter amount, currency, FX rate and method:
    MethodExtra field
    TT (bank transfer)Bank account — which company account received or sent it
    CashCash safe
    ChequeCheque — pick an existing one or create it here
    Offset · Credit Notenone
    The amount is spread over the invoice items from the top down. You can change the share per item."Line added". "Not yet allocated" goes down. One payment can have lines on several invoices.
  4. When the lines equal the declared total ("Fully allocated"), click Confirm.status CONFIRMED. The person's balance and the invoice's "Paid to date" update. The invoice shows "Remaining" or "Overpaid by".

Good to know

  • A DRAFT payment is not counted anywhere. Only CONFIRMED money changes balances.
  • Made a mistake? Reopen the payment to DRAFT, fix the lines, confirm again.

General payment

  • Same steps from the General payments tab, without picking an invoice. Use it for an advance, or money paid to an employee or landlord.

9 · Cheques

Payments › Cheques
Goal: keep a list of every cheque you hold or gave, know when each is due, and record when it clears.
Payments › ChequesNew chequePENDINGMark as paidPAID — money in your account

Add a cheque

  1. Payments › Cheques → New cheque (or create it inside a payment line with method Cheque).
  2. Type: Normal (a real payment) or Security (a guarantee, not for cashing unless needed).
  3. Number, Issuing bank (the bank written on the cheque), Owner (whose cheque it is), Due date, Amount, Currency. Save.the cheque with status PENDING and "Due in N days".

When something happens to the cheque

  1. Open the cheque and change its Status:
    StatusMeaningWhat the app asks
    PENDINGyou hold it, not yet due or not yet presented—
    PAIDthe bank cleared it"Which of your accounts received this money?" — pick the bank or cash safe. For a cheque you gave: which account it left from.
    RETURNEDit bounced—
    EXPIREDdue date passed, never presented—
    CHANGEDreplaced by another cheque—

Find cheques that need action

  1. Use the filters above the list: Due from / Due to, Min / Max amount, and tick Needs action only to see cheques that are due or past due.
  2. Type in the search box to find a cheque by number, owner or bank.

Good to know

  • One cheque can pay lines on several invoices. The cheque page shows "Used on" with the payments that use it.
  • Two different banks can have the same cheque number — that is allowed.

Take care

  • You can move a cheque from any status to any other, because real life can be corrected. But moving to PAID always needs an account, and moving away from PAID removes it.

10 · Transfers · Exchange gain / loss

your own accounts
Goal: record money moved between your own banks and cash safes, and write down what a currency change cost or earned.

Move money between your accounts

TransfersNew transferFrom → ToConfirmTR-0001 confirmed
  1. Transfers → New transfer.
  2. From account and To account — your banks and cash safes from Base Info.
  3. Amount out in the from-currency. If the two accounts have different currencies, enter the Rate; the app shows Amount in. Same currency: rate is 1.
  4. Optional: allocate part of it to an invoice, if this transfer is connected to a deal. It can stay unallocated.
  5. Save, then Confirm. The page says: "Nothing moves until the transfer is confirmed."status CONFIRMED and a number like TR-0001. The Reports › Banks & cash safes tab shows it as money out of one account and into the other.

Exchange gain / loss

Exchange gain/lossNew recordAmount (USD) with + or −Gain or Loss
  1. Use this when a currency change made you earn or lose money — for example you received AED at a worse rate than the invoice rate.
  2. Exchange gain/loss → New record. Enter Date and Amount (USD).
    A positive number is saved as a Gain ("it raises your net"). A negative number is a Loss ("it lowers your net"). Add a note saying why.
  3. Save.a record EGL-0001 marked Gain or Loss, and the Net total at the top of the page.

Good to know

  • A transfer is only between YOUR accounts. Money to or from a person is a payment (section 8).
  • A DRAFT transfer can be edited or cancelled; a CONFIRMED one can only be cancelled.

Simple by design

  • The gain / loss record is just a number and a date. It is not tied to an account or a rate. You decide the figure.

11 · Expenses, revenues and claims

costs, extra income, disputes
Goal: record costs like freight and rent, extra income like commission, and claims about quantity or quality on an invoice.

Expense or revenue document

Expenses / RevenuesInvoice tab or General tabNew … documentAdd category lineTotal in USD
  1. Choose the tab. Invoice expenses = a cost that belongs to one shipment (freight, customs, inspection). General expenses = office costs (rent, salaries, bank fees). Revenues work the same way.
  2. Click New invoice document (pick the invoice — it cannot change later) or New general document. Give it a Title, example "Freight and customs for PP-2026-0001". Save.
  3. Click Add category line: Category (from Base Info), Person (who you paid or who paid you — the shipping company, the landlord …), Amount, Currency, FX rate, optional Cost centre.
    On an invoice document you also split the amount over the goods (the "Share" per line). The app then shows the cost per MT.
  4. Add more lines if needed.the total in USD on the document, and the cost per MT on each goods line. The person's balance changes by the amount.

Claim

ClaimsSale claims / Purchase claimsNew claimPick invoice + itemsClaim amount on the person's account
  1. A sale claim is a buyer complaining to you (goods short or bad → you owe them). A purchase claim is you complaining to a supplier (→ they owe you).
  2. Claims → the right tab → New claim. Select invoice — a confirmed one. The party fills in from the invoice.
  3. Title, Claim type: Quantity (weight short) or Quality. Currency, FX rate, Date.
  4. In Invoice items, enter the claim amount for each affected goods line. Save.the claim total in USD, and the person's balance changed by that amount.

Good to know

  • Documents and claims are not deleted; use Cancel. A cancelled one is ignored everywhere.
  • The Reports › Expenses tab groups costs by category, cost centre and person.

Categories

  • A line can only use a category of the same kind: an "Invoice" category on an invoice document, a "General" category on a general document. If the category you need is missing, add it in Base Info.

14 · For customers: My Account

the customer portal
Goal: a customer signs in and sees their own account — nothing else.
Customer signs inMy AccountBalance · open invoices · payments · contractsRead only

What the customer does

  1. Go to https://erp.metal-uae.com and sign in with the login you gave them.only one page: My Account. Every other page is hidden, and typing another address brings them back here.
  2. At the top: a standing badge — In good standing, Action needed (something overdue) or No activity yet.
  3. Cards: Outstanding balance, Overdue, Available credit, Settlement rate, Avg. days to settle, their payment terms, credit utilization.
  4. Charts: receivables aging, paid vs. outstanding, invoiced vs. collected.
  5. Tables: Open invoices with due date and days overdue, Payments, Contracts. When everything is paid: "No open invoices — you're all settled."

What you do to set it up (manager)

  1. Persons → open the customer → Edit person → tick Customer portal access → Save. The list shows a Portal badge on that person.
  2. Users → make sure there is a user with role Customer (section 15). Give them the email and password.

Good to know

  • The portal is read only. The customer cannot change anything.
  • They see the same numbers you see on their Person page.

Only one

  • Only one person can have portal access at a time. If you tick it on another person, it moves.
  • If you deactivate that person, the portal access is removed. The customer then sees "Account unavailable".

15 · Users, settings, and error messages

for managers

Users

  1. Users → New user: Name, Email (this is their login), Role, Initial password. Save. Tell them the password; they can change it later.
  2. Forgot password? Open the user → Reset password → type a new one.
  3. Someone leaves? Open the user → Deactivate. They cannot sign in anymore. You cannot deactivate yourself.
  4. Your own password: click your name at the top right → Change password.

Roles: Staff = daily operations, no money pages · Manager = everything · CEO = Executive, Reports, Settings, Users · Customer = My Account only. The full page-by-page table is in section 16.

Settings

  1. Theme, Language, Base reporting currency, FX rate (AED per 1 USD), IQD exchange rate (defaults for new forms), Company name (printed on documents). Danger zone — Load sample data / Wipe all data are for testing only; on the real system they are switched off.

Error messages and what to do

MessageMeaningWhat to do
Incorrect email or passwordlogin failedcheck both; ask a manager to reset the password
Quantity exceeds the contract's remaining uninvoiced amountthis contract line is already used by other documentslower the quantity, or cancel the other document
Insufficient stocknot enough of that product in that warehousereceive goods first, or choose another warehouse
A converted document already existsyou cannot convert or cancel because a newer document is linkedwork on the newer document; cancel it first if needed
Cancel the linked warehouse document firstthe invoice already moved stockWarehouse › Documents → cancel the GRN / GDN, then cancel the invoice
Couldn't save changes / red banner at the topthe server did not accept or receive the changecheck your internet, try again; if the banner stays, tell the administrator

Golden rules

  • Set up Base Info and Persons first. Then contracts. Then documents. Then payments.
  • DRAFT = you can still change it and it counts for nothing. CONFIRMED = locked and counted.
  • Never delete — cancel or deactivate. History stays complete.

In simple words

  • CEO — 4 pages. Sees results and manages logins; does not enter contracts or payments.
  • Manager — 19 pages. The only role that can touch money: payments, cheques, transfers, expenses, revenues, claims.
  • Staff — 9 pages. Creates and confirms trade documents, but sees no money page. Even the expenses card on an invoice is hidden.
  • Customer — 1 page, read-only.

How it is enforced

  • At sign-in the server sends each user their list of allowed pages. The menu shows only those; typing another address sends you to your home page.
  • The server checks the permission again on every save, so nobody can write to a page they cannot see.
  • Roles are fixed — no custom roles, no per-user exceptions. A Manager or the CEO changes a user's role in Users.
